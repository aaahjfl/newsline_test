# Data License and Reuse Notes

The NewsLine dataset files in this directory are released for research and educational use.

The dataset contains metadata and derived annotations produced for the NewsLine project, including article titles, source names, URLs, normalized time fields, parser labels, and optional noise/order labels. It does not contain full article body text.

Original article titles, URLs, source names, and publisher identities may remain subject to the rights and terms of their original publishers. Reusers should cite or link to the original publisher URLs where appropriate and should not imply ownership of the underlying news articles.
